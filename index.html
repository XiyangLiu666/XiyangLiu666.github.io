Biography

Xiyang Liu is currently pursuing the Ph.D. degree in Information and Communications Engineering at Xidian University, Xi'an, China, under the supervision of Prof. Min Sheng. He is a member of the State Key Laboratory of Integrated Services Networks (ISN), Xidian University. He is also a Visiting Ph.D. Candidate with the SIGCOM Research Group, Interdisciplinary Centre for Security, Reliability and Trust (SnT), University of Luxembourg, under the supervision of Prof. Symeon Chatzinotas. He received the M.E. degree from Xi'an Electronic Engineering Research Institute, Xi'an, China, in 2023, and the B.E. degree from Xidian University, Xi'an, China, in 2020.

I am enthusiastic about exploring new ideas and especially welcome interdisciplinary academic exchanges and collaborations. Beyond research, I enjoy adventure, traveling, and rock music. I am always happy to connect with fellow researchers, so please feel free to reach out to me at xiyangliu@stu.xidian.edu.cn or xiyang.liu@uni.lu.

Research Interests

Satellite communication, large-scale LEO satellite networks, resilience and reliability of satellite networks.
